GridPix Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE ADS GO LIVE]
Developer: Blanks Studio
App: GridPix (Android package com.blanksstudio.gridpix)

Summary

GridPix is a puzzle game that works offline. Blanks Studio does not collect, store or share any personal information and has no user accounts or analytics. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below. Buying "Remove ads" or the Everything bundle turns ads off.

Data stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, solved pictures, statistics, hint balance, daily streak and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. If you have Android backup enabled, Android may include this data in your device backup; Blanks Studio has no access to it.

In-app purchases

Purchases (hint packs, picture packs, the Large grids unlock, Remove ads and the Everything bundle) are processed by Google Play. Google provides the app with a purchase receipt so that the item can be unlocked and restored on your device. The app does not receive your name, email address or payment details. Google's handling of your payment data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The free version of GridPix shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, a full-screen ad between puzzles (at most after every third solved puzzle and not more than once every four minutes), and an optional video you can choose to watch for a free hint. No ads are shown on the first day after installing.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), information about your device and how you interact with ads, and crash data from the ad software. This data is collected and processed by Google, not Blanks Studio. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, GridPix asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Permissions

GridPix asks for the notification permission only if you turn on the optional daily puzzle reminder. The reminder is created on your device and contains no personal data. Internet access is used by Google Play to process and restore purchases and show the optional "rate this app" prompt, and by Google AdMob to load ads. The advertising ID permission is used only by Google AdMob.

Sharing

If you tap "Share picture", GridPix creates an image of your solved picture and opens Android's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with. You can hide the Share button in Settings.

Children

GridPix is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Blanks Studio collects no personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.
