GridPix Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026
Developer: Blanks Studio
App: GridPix (Android package com.blanksstudio.gridpix)

Summary

GridPix is an offline puzzle game. It does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network features other than Google Play's in-app purchase system.

Data stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, solved pictures, statistics, hint balance, daily streak and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. If you have Android backup enabled, Android may include this data in your device backup; Blanks Studio has no access to it.

In-app purchases

Purchases (hint packs, picture packs, the Large grids unlock and the Everything bundle) are processed by Google Play. Google provides the app with a purchase receipt so that the item can be unlocked and restored on your device. The app does not receive your name, email address or payment details. Google's handling of your payment data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

GridPix requests no special Android permissions. Internet access is used only by the Google Play Billing library to process and restore purchases.

Children

GridPix is suitable for a general audience. Because it collects no data, no personal information is collected from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
